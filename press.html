Short description

Nalbaki Games is an independent studio making original games that run in the browser, free to play on a laptop or a phone, and building custom games, playable ads and portal-ready builds for clients.

Long description

Nalbaki Games is an independent studio that makes games for the browser: no install, no account, playable on a laptop or a phone seconds after you open the link. Its first three games each try a different genre: Parking Lot, a race against three bots to the only free space in a busy car park; Last Commit Standing, a 20-player battle royale you play solo against nineteen bots; and Floor 42, a side-on office duel where the first to five knockouts wins.

The studio works with modern tools, including AI-assisted coding, and measures what it ships: the Parking Lot portal build is 347 KB over the wire and reaches the race screen in 2.5 seconds on fast 4G.
