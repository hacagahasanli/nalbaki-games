Pewfield: a free 1v1 shootout for 2 players or vs a bot

Pewfield is a fast side-on 1v1 shootout at a sun-baked company field day. Pick one of four fighters, each with a trick of their own, grab the guns that turn up around the arena and knock your rival out five times first. Play the bot on three levels or a friend on the same keyboard, free in your browser. Pewfield was called Floor 42 until November 2026.

Fight a bot → 2 players, one keyboard

How to play

  1. PickFour fighters: BRIX, ORMO, ZUZU and TAVO, each with a trick of their own.
  2. Grab a gunEveryone starts with a pistol that never runs dry. An SMG, Uzi, shotgun, sniper rail, rockets and the bomb turn up around the arena.
  3. Knock them outDash through shots, hide behind crates, and Force Push your rival: it even sends their bullets back. First to five KOs wins.

Controls

1 player
A / D move · W jump (again in the air to double jump) · S drop through a walkway · Space shoot (your gun aims itself) · Shift dash · Q Force Push · F pick up a gun · G throw the bomb · T mouse aim · Esc pause
2 players
Player 1: A / D move, W jump, S drop, F shoot, G push, H dash. Player 2: ← / → move, ↑ jump, ↓ drop, ; shoot, L push, K dash. Guns are picked up by walking over them.
Gamepad
2 players only: one pad plays Player 2, two pads play both. Stick or d-pad move, A jump, X or RT shoot, B or LB push, RB or LT dash, Start pause.

Questions about Pewfield

Is Pewfield free?

Yes, it is free to play in the browser.

Do I need to install it?

No. It runs in the browser on a laptop, phone or tablet.

Can two people play on one computer?

Yes. Pick 2 PLAYER in the main menu: one player uses WASD, the other the arrow keys, first to 3, 5 or 7 KOs. One or two gamepads work too.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes, against the bot, with touch controls in landscape. Two players on one phone need two gamepads.

Is Pewfield the same game as Floor 42?

Yes. Floor 42 was renamed Pewfield in November 2026. The office map is still called FLOOR 42.
