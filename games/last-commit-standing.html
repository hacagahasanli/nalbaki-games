Last Commit Standing: a 4-minute battle royale in your browser

Last Commit Standing is a free browser battle royale by Nalbaki Games. Twenty players drop from a cargo plane, loot guns and armor, and fight inside a closing zone until one is left; today you play solo against 19 bots. A round lasts about 4 minutes and runs on a laptop or a phone with no install.

Drop in, play free →

How to play

  1. DropJump from the cargo plane, free-fall, and steer the chute to where you want to land.
  2. LootGuns, armor, ammo, med kits and bombs lie in houses, barns and the port.
  3. OutlastThe zone closes phase by phase. Outside it you lose health every second.
