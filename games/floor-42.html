Floor 42: a 1v1 office shootout, first to 5 KOs

Floor 42 is a free browser duel by Nalbaki Games. Two fighters trade fire across an office at night; your gun aims itself, so it comes down to footwork. Play against a bot (easy, normal or hard) or online 1v1 with a friend or a quick match, first to 5 knockouts, on a laptop or a phone in landscape.

Fight a bot → Play online 1v1

How to play

  1. PickSame guns for everyone. Speed, size, jumps and shove weight differ.
  2. Grab a gunSMG, shotgun, railgun or rocket launcher turn up on the floor.
  3. Knock them outEveryone has the same health. A pistol shot up close takes half of it, a rocket takes all of it. First to five KOs wins.

Questions about Floor 42

Yes, it is free to play.
