Parking Lot: race three bots for the last free bay

Parking Lot is a free browser game by Nalbaki Games, an independent studio. You race three bots through a supermarket car park to park first, with all four wheels inside the lines. A round takes 1 to 3 minutes, there are 6 stages from the open lot to the roof, and it runs on a laptop or a phone with no install.

Play Parking Lot →

How to play

  1. RaceThree beeps and the handbrakes come off. Somewhere in the lot, one bay is empty.
  2. ParkAll four corners inside the lines, square to the bay. Then hold still.
  3. ClimbPark first to win the stage. Every win opens one more floor.

Touch anything and you are out.
