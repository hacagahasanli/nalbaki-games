Quick answers

Do I need an account or a download?

No. Every game runs in the browser from a link. Settings and progress are saved in your browser on that device.

Can I play on a phone?

Yes. All three games have touch controls. Hold the phone sideways for the best view. Control size and position can be changed in Settings.

The game runs slowly. What can I do?

Open Settings, then Graphics, and pick a lower quality. Closing other tabs helps too. If it is still slow, report it with your device and browser so we can tune it.

Can I change the keys?

Yes. Settings, then Controls: click an action and press the new key. Gamepads work as well.

My progress disappeared.

Progress lives in your browser storage. Private windows and clearing site data remove it. On a game portal it follows that portal’s account instead.

A bug report we can fix fast

  1. Which game, and which level or mode.
  2. Your device and browser, for example “iPhone 13, Safari”.
  3. What you did just before it happened.
  4. What you saw, and what you expected instead.
  5. A screenshot or a short screen recording, if you can.
