Parking Lot Hazefall Pewfield

Featured Driving race · Free to play

Four cars. One free space. Not a scratch allowed.

Play Parking Lot → About Parking Lot Speed and specs

It is the end of a long day and the supermarket car park is full, except for one bay. You want it. So do three bots. Hunt the aisles and the decks, slip past the shoppers, and get all four corners inside the lines first. Touch anything and you are out.

Genre
Careful-driving race
Mode
Solo: you against 3 bots
Length
Rounds of 1 to 3 minutes, 6 stages from the open lot to the roof
Plays on
Laptop or phone, in the browser
  1. RaceThree beeps and the handbrakes come off. Somewhere in the lot, one bay is empty.
  2. ParkAll four corners inside the lines, square to the bay. Then hold still.
  3. ClimbPark first to win the stage. Every win opens one more floor.

Battle royale · Free to play

Twenty drop in. One walks out.

Drop in, play free → About Hazefall Speed and specs

Jump from the plane, steer your parachute and land with a pistol and one magazine. Loot the farms and the river port, swim the river, hide in the tall grass, and stay ahead of the zone as it closes in. The last one standing takes the round.

Genre
Top-down battle royale
Mode
20 players a round: solo against 19 bots, rooms with friends coming soon
Arsenal
6 weapons from pistol to rockets, 4 fighters with a super move each
Length
About 4 minutes a round
Plays on
Laptop or phone, in the browser
  1. DropJump from the cargo plane, free-fall, and steer the chute to where you want to land.
  2. LootGuns, armor, ammo, med kits and bombs lie in houses, barns and the port.
  3. OutlastThe zone closes phase by phase. Outside it you lose health every second.

1v1 duel · Free to play

Two fighters. One field. First to five KOs.

Fight a bot → 2 players, one keyboard

A fast side-on 1v1 shootout at a sun-baked company field day. Your gun aims itself, so the fight is all about moving, jumping, dashing and timing your shots. Hide behind crates and boardwalks, and shove your rival with a Force Push that even sends their bullets back.

Genre
Side-on 1v1 shooter
Mode
Against a bot (easy, normal or hard), or 2 players on one keyboard
To win
First to 5 KOs
Plays on
Laptop, or phone in landscape against the bot
  1. PickFour fighters: BRIX, ORMO, ZUZU and TAVO, each with a trick of their own.
  2. Grab a gunEveryone starts with a pistol that never runs dry. An SMG, Uzi, shotgun, sniper rail, rockets and the bomb turn up around the arena.
  3. Knock them outDash through shots, hide behind crates, and Force Push your rival: it even sends their bullets back. First to five KOs wins.
