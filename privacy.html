Privacy

We count visits so we know which games people enjoy. We do not use cookies on our pages, we never keep your IP address, and there is no account to make.

What we count

  • Which pages and games are opened, and whether a game loads and how fast.
  • Rounds played and how they ended, how long a visit lasts, and whether you come back another day.
  • Your device type (phone, tablet or computer), operating system and browser family, and your screen size in three rough steps.
  • Your country, which Cloudflare works out from your connection. We never see or keep the IP address behind it.
  • The website you came from (its name only, never the full address) and the campaign tag on a link, if there is one.

What we never do

  • No cookies on the public pages, no advertising, no tracking across other websites.
  • No names, emails, accounts or IP addresses in our records.
  • No selling or sharing of the numbers. They stay on our own server, and only the studio owner can open them.

How it works

Your browser keeps a random number, a label with no meaning outside this counter, for up to 13 months, so we can tell a new player from a returning one. A second random number lasts for one visit in one tab.

The numbers go to our own server at stats.nalbakigames.com. Raw records are kept for up to 13 months and then deleted; only totals with no ids stay. When the game runs on a game portal such as CrazyGames, it sends nothing to us.

Switch it off

Counting is already off if your browser sends Global Privacy Control, and for automated browsers. You can also turn it off for this device and browser here. It also clears the random number we stored.

Another way: open any page of ours with ?nostats at the end of the address.

Questions or a request about your data: hello@nalbakigames.com. Last updated 21 November 2026.
