Before you ask

Who actually does the work?

One developer, working with modern tools including AI-assisted coding. Every build is reviewed, tested and played before it reaches you. There is no team to hand you off to, and no subcontractors.

Who owns the code?

You do, once the final payment is made. You get the full source, not just a build.

Can you do online multiplayer?

Yes. Pewfield has a server-authoritative online duel that runs a 60 Hz simulation (public play is paused for now), and Hazefall has online rooms of up to ten people, filled to twenty with bots. Multiplayer is quoted separately because it needs a server and more testing.

How do I pay?

Through Upwork or Contra if you prefer a marketplace with escrow, or by direct invoice. Larger projects are split into milestones.

What do you need from me?

A short brief, your logo and brand colours, any art you already have, and one person who can answer questions within a day.
