Tell us about your game

Email is the fastest way to reach the studio. You get a reply from the person who will build it.

hello@nalbakigames.com

A good first email

  1. What the player does, in one or two sentences.
  2. Where it will run: your site, an ad network, a game portal, an app.
  3. Which package is closest, if any.
  4. The date you need it by, and any fixed budget.
  5. Links to your brand, your game or a reference you like.
Write a brief

Other reasons to write

  • Portals and publishers: all three games can be prepared for your platform. Ask for builds or a call.
  • Press and creators: the press kit has logos, screenshots and descriptions. Ask for anything else.
  • Players: found a bug or have an idea for a game? Send it.

You can also hire the studio through Upwork or Contra. Ask for the profile link.
